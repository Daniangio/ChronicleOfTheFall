# Chronicles of the Fall

## Anonymous Council Engine — Prototype Rules v0.2

### Design Goal

Chronicles of the Fall is a semi-cooperative game about a declining Empire shaped by factions with incompatible historical visions.

The Empire is shared. Its cities, buildings, resources, tags, crises, and pillars belong to everyone. However, players secretly influence what enters the Empire. Each Era, every player commits one card face-down into the machinery of government. These cards are shuffled and revealed anonymously, so the Empire evolves through a mixture of public necessity, hidden ambition, and plausible deniability.

Players win only when the Empire collapses and their Hidden Agenda is satisfied.

---

# 1. Core Concepts

## 1.1 The Empire

The Empire is represented by:

* **Cities**
* **Buildings**
* **Global Resources**
* **Permanent Tags**
* **Three Pillars**: Treasury, Stability, Morale

The Empire collapses immediately when any Pillar reaches 0.

## 1.2 Tags

Tags are permanent structural properties of the Empire.

Examples:

* Military
* Sanitary
* Food
* Industry
* Commerce
* Faith
* Knowledge
* Authority
* Bureaucracy
* Urban
* Frontier

Tags are not spent. They are used for:

* satisfying building requirements;
* resolving crises;
* checking Hidden Agendas;
* triggering card effects.

A building can provide one or more tags once built.

Example:

```text
Garrison
Provides: Military
```

Once built, the Garrison permanently adds one Military tag to the Empire unless destroyed.

## 1.3 Resources

Resources are temporary productive capacity generated by the Empire each Era.

Default common resources:

* Labor
* Material
* Wealth

Optional rare resources for advanced cards:

* Arms
* Medicine
* Faith
* Knowledge
* Authority

Resources are spent to build cards or resolve card effects. Unlike tags, resources are consumed.

A card may require both resources and tags.

Example:

```text
Public Baths
Requirement: Urban
Cost: 1 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth
Provides: Sanitary
```

The Empire must already have the required tag or city condition, and the listed resources must be available in the Global Resource Pool.

# 2. Components

## 2.1 Empire Deck

The Empire Deck contains all cards players may draw and play.

Card types include:

* Buildings
* Cities / City Expansions
* Events
* Political cards, treated as Events unless later separated

The Empire Deck discard pile is reshuffled into a new Empire Deck when the deck is empty.

## 2.2 Crisis Deck

There is no Event Queue in this prototype.

Instead, if using external crises, reveal and resolve one Crisis card during the Crisis Phase. The Crisis Deck represents disasters outside direct player control.

For the first test version:

```text
Era 1: no Crisis.
Era 2 onward: reveal and resolve one Crisis per Era.
```

This can be made harsher by resolving a Crisis from Era 1.

## 2.3 Hidden Agendas

Each player receives one Hidden Agenda during setup.

Agendas are revealed only when the Empire collapses.

A player wins if their Agenda condition is satisfied at collapse. If multiple players satisfy their Agenda, use the tie-breaker defined by the Agenda cards or by the general rulebook.

---

# 3. Setup

## 3.1 Pillars

Set the three Pillars to their starting values:

```text
Treasury: 5
Stability: 5
Morale: 5
```

If any Pillar reaches 0, the game ends immediately.

## 3.2 Starting City

Place the Capital in play.

Prototype Capital:

```text
Capital
Tags: Urban, Bureaucracy
Slots: 4
Production: 2 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth
Storage: 2 resources
```

The Capital is a City and does not occupy one of its own building slots.

## 3.3 Starting Hands

Create a predefined Base Card Pool.

Each player receives:

```text
3 random Base cards
2 random Empire Deck cards
```

Each player therefore starts with 5 cards.

Suggested Base cards:

```text
Farm
Cost: 1 Labor
Provides: Food
Production: +1 Labor
```

```text
Quarry
Cost: 1 Labor
Provides: Industry
Production: +2 Material
```

```text
Garrison
Cost: 1 Material
Provides: Military
Production: +1 Arms
```

```text
Market
Cost: 1 Labor, 1 Material
Provides: Commerce
Production: +1 Wealth
```

```text
Shrine
Cost: 1 Material
Provides: Faith
Production: +1 Faith
```

```text
Clinic
Cost: 1 Labor, 1 Wealth
Provides: Sanitary
Production: +1 Medicine
```

The exact number of Base cards in the pool should scale with player count. For example, in a 4-player game, prepare 12 Base cards and deal 3 to each player.

## 3.4 Scheme Slots

Each player has 2 private Scheme Slots.

Scheme Slots allow players to preserve important cards for later Eras.

Cards in Scheme Slots:

* are private;
* may be played during the Plotting Phase;
* do not count against the hand limit;
* may be swapped with cards from hand during Cleanup.

## 3.5 Hand Limit

The hand limit is 5 cards.

If a player ever has more than 5 cards in hand after drawing and scheming, they must discard down to 5.

---

# 4. Ministries

Ministries are public roles. They give small but important powers.

They also create behavioral clues. A player holding a Ministry has an obvious incentive to play certain kinds of cards, but this does not prove they played any specific card.

## 4.1 Ministry Cards

There are five offices:

1. Minister of the Empire
2. Minister of Cities
3. Minister of State
4. Minister of Health & Harvest
5. Minister of War

## 4.2 Assigning Ministries

At the beginning of each Era, assign Ministries before Suspicion tokens are placed.

### Step 1 — Rotate Minister of the Empire

The Minister of the Empire passes clockwise each Era.

The Minister of the Empire is the current turn-order leader and default decision-maker.

### Step 2 — Draft the Other Ministries

Starting with the player to the left of the Minister of the Empire and proceeding clockwise, players draft the remaining Ministry cards one at a time until all four non-Empire Ministries have been assigned.

A player may hold more than one Ministry if the draft wraps around.

In a 5-player game, each player usually holds one office.

In a 4-player game, the Minister of the Empire will usually also hold one additional Ministry.

In a 3-player game, two players will usually hold two offices and one player will hold one office.

## 4.3 Missing Ministers

Some card effects may temporarily remove, block, or disable a Ministry.

If a decision must be made by a missing Minister, the Minister of the Empire makes that decision instead.

If the Minister of the Empire is also unable to decide, the next player clockwise decides.

---

# 5. Ministry Powers

## 5.1 Minister of the Empire

The Minister of the Empire:

* leads turn order;
* breaks ties unless a card says otherwise;
* makes decisions for missing Ministers;
* resolves any ambiguous timing disputes during the Era.

If multiple effects happen at the same time and no rule defines the order, the Minister of the Empire chooses the order.

## 5.2 Minister of Cities

The Minister of Cities places all Buildings and Cities.

When a Building is successfully built, the Minister of Cities chooses the legal City where it is placed.

When a new City or City Expansion is built, the Minister of Cities chooses its placement or target, following the card text.

During Storage, the Minister of Cities chooses which leftover resources are stored if the Empire has more leftover resources than storage capacity.

## 5.3 Minister of State

During Cleanup, the Minister of State draws up to 2 cards instead of 1.

They may keep both cards only if this does not exceed the hand limit.

Example:

```text
If the Minister of State has 3 cards in hand, they draw 2 and go to 5.
If they have 4 cards in hand, they draw only 1.
If they already have 5 cards in hand, they draw 0 unless they first discarded or schemed a card.
```

## 5.4 Minister of Health & Harvest

The Minister of Health & Harvest cannot be forced to discard cards from hand or Scheme Slots.

If an Event says “all players discard 1 card,” the Minister of Health & Harvest ignores that effect.

If an Event specifically targets the Minister of Health & Harvest with discard, the discard is ignored.

This protection applies only to forced discard. The Minister may still voluntarily discard cards if a card effect allows or requires voluntary payment.

## 5.5 Minister of War

During Crisis Resolution, the Minister of War may add +1 to the Empire’s defense total.

If the Empire has at least one Military tag, the Minister of War may add +2 instead.

This power may be used once per Era.

---

# 6. Era Structure

Each Era follows this sequence:

```text
1. Ministry Assignment
2. Suspicion Phase
3. Production Phase
4. Queued Project Resolution
5. Plotting Phase
6. Anonymous Reveal Phase
7. Stalled Project Vote
8. Crisis Phase
9. Storage Phase
10. Scheme / Draw / Cleanup
```

---

# 7. Suspicion Phase

At the beginning of each Era, after Ministries are assigned but before cards are committed, each player places one Suspicion token.

Starting with the Minister of the Empire and proceeding clockwise, each player places their Suspicion token on:

* another player;
* or no one.

A player may not place Suspicion on themselves.

Suspicion tokens are public.

## 7.1 Suspicion Thresholds

After all Suspicion tokens are placed, count how many each player received.

```text
0–1 Suspicion:
No effect.

2 Suspicion:
That player must commit their card face-up this Era.

3+ Suspicion:
That player must commit their card face-up and cannot commit an Event card this Era.
```

If a player with 3+ Suspicion has only Event cards in hand and Scheme Slots, they reveal their hand and Scheme cards to prove it, then commit no card this Era.

## 7.2 Clearing Suspicion

All Suspicion tokens are removed at the end of the Era.

Suspicion does not persist between Eras unless a card specifically says otherwise.

---

# 8. Production Phase

During Production, the Empire generates resources.

Add together:

* production from all Cities;
* production from all Buildings;
* resources stored from previous Eras.

Place these resources into the Global Resource Pool.

Resources in the Global Resource Pool are not owned by any player.

Example:

```text
Capital produces 2 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth.
Quarry produces 2 Material.
Market produces 1 Wealth.

Total Global Resource Pool:
2 Labor
3 Material
2 Wealth
```

---

# 9. Queued Project Resolution

Some Stalled Projects may have been voted to continue from the previous Era.

These are called Queued Projects.

After Production and before new cards are committed, resolve Queued Projects from left to right.

For each Queued Project:

* if all requirements are satisfied and all resources are available, build it and spend the resources;
* if even one requirement or resource is missing, move it to the Stalled Row for this Era.

Queued Projects that fail do not automatically remain queued. They must survive the Stalled Project Vote again.

---

# 10. Plotting Phase

Each player must commit one card, unless prevented by Suspicion or a card effect.

A player may commit:

* one card from hand;
* or one card from a Scheme Slot.

Cards are committed face-down unless the player has enough Suspicion to be forced face-up.

## 10.1 Face-Up Commitment

If a player must commit face-up, the card remains visibly associated with that player.

For physical play, use a face-down placeholder card in the anonymous stack. Keep the real card face-up in front of the player. When the placeholder is revealed, resolve that player’s face-up card.

For digital play, the interface can simply randomize the reveal position while preserving ownership visibility.

## 10.2 Anonymous Stack

All face-down committed cards are shuffled together into the Council Stack.

Players should not know which player submitted which face-down card.

---

# 11. Anonymous Reveal Phase

Reveal cards from the Council Stack one at a time.

Resolve each card immediately before revealing the next.

There are three main card types:

```text
Building / City
Event
Special card, if added later
```

For the current prototype, Political cards are treated as Events.

---

# 12. Buildings and Cities

When a Building or City card is revealed, check whether it can be built.

A card can be built only if:

1. all tag or city requirements are satisfied;
2. the Empire has enough resources in the Global Resource Pool;
3. there is a legal city slot or legal placement target.

If all conditions are satisfied, the card is built.

If even one resource is missing, or one requirement is not satisfied, the card is not built and enters the Stalled Row.

## 12.1 Resource Consumption

Resource consumption is all-or-nothing.

```text
If the full cost is available, pay the full cost and build the card.
If even one required resource is missing, pay nothing and move the card to the Stalled Row.
```

Example:

```text
Academy
Requirement: Urban
Cost: 2 Wealth, 1 Knowledge
Provides: Knowledge, Bureaucracy
```

If the Empire has 2 Wealth but no Knowledge, the Academy is not built. No Wealth is spent. The Academy goes to the Stalled Row.

## 12.2 Building Placement

If a Building is successfully built, the Minister of Cities chooses where to place it.

The chosen City must:

* satisfy the card’s requirements;
* have an available building slot;
* obey any special placement rule on the card.

If no legal City exists, the card stalls.

## 12.3 Cities

City cards create new City zones.

A City card may have:

* tags;
* slots;
* production;
* storage;
* special rules.

Example:

```text
Frontier Town
Type: City
Requirement: none
Cost: 1 Labor, 1 Material
Tags: Frontier
Slots: 3
Production: +1 Labor
Storage: 1
```

Cities do not occupy building slots.

---

# 13. Events

Events resolve immediately when revealed.

Events may:

* add resources;
* remove resources;
* modify Pillars;
* destroy Buildings;
* discard cards from hands or Scheme Slots;
* affect Suspicion;
* affect Crisis resolution;
* alter storage;
* modify future card costs;
* trigger conditional effects.

Events do not enter the Stalled Row.

If an Event has a condition and the condition is not satisfied, follow the card text. If the card provides no alternate effect, discard it with no effect.

Example:

```text
Tax Riots
Type: Event
Effect: Add 2 Wealth to the Global Resource Pool.
Then, if Morale is 4 or lower, lose 1 Stability.
```

Example:

```text
Border Panic
Type: Event
Effect: If the Empire has fewer than 2 Military tags, lose 1 Stability.
Otherwise, add 1 Arms to the Global Resource Pool.
```

Example:

```text
Court Scandal
Type: Event
Effect: The player with the most Suspicion discards 1 card from hand or Scheme.
If tied, no effect.
```

If Court Scandal targets the Minister of Health & Harvest, that player ignores the discard effect.

---

# 14. Stalled Project Vote

During the Anonymous Reveal Phase, any unbuilt Building or City card is placed face-up into the Stalled Row in reveal order.

After all committed cards and Events have been resolved, players vote on which Stalled Projects should remain alive.

## 14.1 Voting

Each player has 1 public vote.

A player may vote for:

* one Stalled Project;
* or none.

Votes are public.

## 14.2 Keeping Stalled Projects

Any Stalled Project with at least 2 votes becomes a Queued Project for the next Era.

Queued Projects keep their relative order from the Stalled Row.

All other Stalled Projects are discarded.

For a 5-player game, the threshold may later be increased to 3 votes if too many projects survive, but the default prototype threshold is 2.

---

# 15. Crisis Phase

There is no Event Queue.

Starting from Era 2, reveal one Crisis card from the Crisis Deck and resolve it immediately.

A Crisis card has:

* defense requirement;
* relevant tags;
* success effect;
* failure effect.

Example:

```text
Plague
Requires: 3 Sanitary
Success: +1 Morale
Failure: -2 Morale
```

To resolve a Crisis:

1. Count the Empire’s relevant permanent tags.
2. Add any temporary modifiers.
3. The Minister of War may use their power.
4. Compare the total to the Crisis requirement.
5. Apply Success or Failure.

Example:

```text
The Empire has 2 Sanitary tags.
A Plague requires 3 Sanitary.
The Minister of War cannot help unless using the generic +1 defense rule.
If the Minister of War adds +1, the Empire reaches 3 and succeeds.
```

The old Secret Pitch system is not used in this prototype. The anonymous Council Stack now carries the hidden-action role.

---

# 16. Storage Phase

After Crisis Resolution, unused resources in the Global Resource Pool are discarded unless stored.

Each City has storage capacity.

Default:

```text
Each City can store up to 2 resources total.
```

Buildings may increase storage.

Example:

```text
Warehouse
Provides: Industry
Storage: +2 Material or Wealth
```

If there are more leftover resources than available storage, the Minister of Cities chooses which resources are stored.

Stored resources become available again during the next Production Phase.

Example:

```text
The Empire has 1 Labor, 2 Wealth, and 1 Medicine left.
The Capital has Storage 2.
The Minister of Cities chooses to store 1 Wealth and 1 Medicine.
The remaining Labor and Wealth are discarded.
```

---

# 17. Scheme / Draw / Cleanup

At the end of each Era, each player may manage Scheme Slots.

A player may:

* place one card from hand into an empty Scheme Slot;
* or swap one card from hand with one card already in a Scheme Slot;
* or do nothing.

Each player then draws 1 card.

The Minister of State draws up to 2 cards instead of 1, without exceeding the hand limit.

After drawing, each player discards down to the hand limit of 5.

Cards discarded from hand or Scheme Slots go to the discard pile.

Built Buildings and Cities stay in play unless destroyed.

Destroyed cards go to the discard pile unless the card says otherwise.

When the Empire Deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to create a new Empire Deck.

# 18. Example Era

## Situation

The Empire has:

```text
Capital
Tags: Urban, Bureaucracy
Slots: 4
Production: 2 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth
Storage: 2

Quarry
Provides: Industry
Production: +2 Material
```

Pillars:

```text
Treasury: 5
Stability: 5
Morale: 4
```

Production creates:

```text
2 Labor
3 Material
1 Wealth
```

## Suspicion

Four players place Suspicion tokens.

Player A receives 2 Suspicion.

Player A must commit their card face-up.

## Plotting

Players commit cards.

Player A commits face-up:

```text
Public Baths
Requirement: Urban
Cost: 1 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth
Provides: Sanitary
Built Effect: +1 Morale
```

The other three players commit face-down.

The face-down cards are shuffled. Player A’s face-up card is assigned a random placeholder position.

## Reveal

First revealed card:

```text
Tax Riots
Event
Add 2 Wealth.
If Morale is 4 or lower, lose 1 Stability.
```

The Empire gains 2 Wealth, then loses 1 Stability.

New resources:

```text
2 Labor
3 Material
3 Wealth
```

Second revealed card:

```text
Garrison
Cost: 1 Material
Provides: Military
Production: +1 Arms
```

The Garrison can be built. Spend 1 Material. The Minister of Cities places it in the Capital.

Remaining resources:

```text
2 Labor
2 Material
3 Wealth
```

Third revealed card is Player A’s Public Baths.

The Empire has Urban and enough resources. Spend 1 Labor, 1 Material, 1 Wealth. Public Baths are built in the Capital. Morale increases by 1.

Remaining resources:

```text
1 Labor
1 Material
2 Wealth
```

Fourth revealed card:

```text
Academy
Requirement: Urban, Knowledge
Cost: 2 Wealth
Provides: Knowledge, Bureaucracy
Production: +1 Knowledge
```

The Empire has Urban but does not yet have a Knowledge tag. The Academy cannot be built. No resources are spent. The Academy enters the Stalled Row.

## Stalled Vote

Only Academy is stalled.

Each player votes publicly.

Two players vote for Academy. It becomes a Queued Project for the next Era.

## Crisis

A Crisis is revealed:

```text
Border Raid
Requires: 2 Military
Success: gain 1 Wealth next Era
Failure: -1 Stability
```

The Empire has 1 Military from the new Garrison.

The Minister of War uses their power. Because the Empire has at least one Military tag, they add +2 defense.

Total defense is 3. The Empire succeeds.

## Storage

Remaining resources:

```text
1 Labor
1 Material
2 Wealth
```

The Capital can store 2 resources.

The Minister of Cities stores:

```text
1 Material
1 Wealth
```

The remaining Labor and Wealth are discarded.

## Cleanup

Players may Scheme or swap one card.

Each player draws 1 card.

The Minister of State draws up to 2 cards instead, without exceeding hand size 5.

# 19. Prototype Tuning Targets

During testing, track:

```text
Average Eras per hour
Number of Buildings successfully built per Era
Number of cards entering Stalled Row per Era
How often Stalled Projects survive voting
How often players use Suspicion correctly
Whether players can infer likely culprits without certainty
Whether Ministries feel worth taking
Whether Events feel disruptive but not purely destructive
Whether the Empire collapses too early or too late
```

The current prototype should not yet add:

```text
Public market
Private resources
Private buildings
Resource lanes
Risk icons
Event queue
Duty mechanic
Complex ministry elections
```

Those can be added later only if the simple anonymous engine proves fun.
