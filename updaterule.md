# Updated Crisis System

## Design Intent

Edicts are political tools.

Crisis cards are political burdens.

A player may choose when to unleash a Crisis, but cannot avoid Crisis forever. This creates a slow, private pressure clock: every faction is carrying disasters, threats, scandals, revolts, invasions, or social breakdowns that must eventually enter imperial history.

---

# Crisis Deck

Crisis cards are not shuffled into the normal Empire Deck.

Instead, create a separate **Crisis Deck**.

The Crisis Deck contains only Event cards with subtype **Crisis**.

Crisis cards are drawn at fixed points during the game and are kept by players until played.

---

# Crisis Burden

Crisis cards in a player’s hand or Scheme Slots are private.
Crisis cards cannot be discarded during normal hand cleanup.
Crisis cards can be Schemed.
When a player plays a Crisis, it resolves like an Event and then goes to the discard pile.

---

# Setup Crisis

During setup, after each player receives their starting hand, each player draws 1 card from the Crisis Deck.

That Crisis is added to their hand.

This means every player begins the game with one private source of future pressure.

---

# Crisis Intake Step

At the end of every 5th Era, each player draws 1 additional Crisis card.

This happens during the End Phase, before normal hand refill.

Crisis Intake occurs at the end of:

Era 5
Era 10
Era 15
Era 20

and so on, if the game lasts that long.

Most games should probably collapse before Era 15, but the rule remains consistent.

---

# Updated End Phase Order

Replace the old Refill / Cleanup phase with the following precise End Phase sequence.

## 1. Storage Step

After the Condition Phase, unused resources in the Global Resource Pool may be stored.

The Minister of Cities chooses which leftover resources are stored, up to the Empire’s available storage capacity.

All unstored resources are discarded.

## 2. Crisis Intake Step

If the completed Era number is divisible by 5, each player draws 1 Crisis card from the Crisis Deck.

The drawn Crisis is added to that player’s hand.

If the Crisis Deck is empty, skip this draw.

## 3. Hand Discard Step

Each player discards all non-Crisis cards
