2. The Empire Deck probably needs staged development, but not fixed openings

Your second problem is the deepest one. The current structure pool has many cool chains, but the link cards are probabilistic. In the catalog, cards like Scribe Room, Scriptorium, Tax Office, Trade Court, and Public Infirmary depend on already having specific tags/resources such as Culture, Science, Diplomacy, Sanitary, Material, Wealth, or Knowledge. If the bridge cards do not appear at the right time, the table remains stuck with Labor, Food, and sometimes Favor.

I would not solve this only with Edicts. Edicts should help, but if Edicts are the main way to access mid-game, then buildings stop feeling like an economy.

The strongest solution is:

Split the Structure economy into two development stages,
but keep variability inside each stage.

Not “same early game every time.” Instead:

Foundation Deck:
early Structures, first tags, first uncommon-resource producers, simple Edicts.

Institution Deck:
advanced Structures, rare tags, Knowledge, capstones, stronger Edicts.

Then use an unlock condition:

At the end of Era 4, or once the Empire has built 5 Structures, shuffle the Institution Deck into the Empire Deck.

This preserves variability because the Foundation Deck can still contain many different early paths:

Industry path
Faith path
Culture path
Sanitary path
Military path
Diplomacy seed path
Science seed path

But it prevents late-game cards from clogging early hands, and it prevents basic bridge cards from being diluted by a huge structure pool.

I would classify cards like this:

Foundation Deck

Cards with:

no required tags
or 1 common tag requirement
cost mostly Labor/Food/Favor
may produce first Material/Wealth/Influence
may provide first Culture/Faith/Industry/Military/Sanitary
very limited Science/Diplomacy

Examples from your current pool that feel Foundation-like:

Farm
Road Camp
Woodcutter / Lumberyard
Stone Quarry
Communal Oven
Envoy House
Healing House
Reliquary Crypt
Street Market
Training Ground
Temple
Town Hall if softened
Institution Deck

Cards with:

2+ tag requirements
Science/Diplomacy requirements
Knowledge costs
Knowledge production
capstone effects
strong pillar effects
multi-resource costs

Examples:

Scriptorium
Trade Court
Public Infirmary
Academy-like cards
Monastery
Observatory
Hospital
War Foundry / Arsenal
advanced Diplomacy/Science cards

This solves the “wrong card at wrong moment” problem while preserving replayability.

4. Cities need to become a public board system, not cards in the deck

I agree that Cities should not be in the Empire Deck. They are too structural. If not drawn, players run out of space; if drawn at the wrong time, they clog hands.

The current rules already define Cities as Development cards that create new zones and Structures as cards that occupy slots. But for the game to work, founding a City should be a separate public action.

I would use a City Charter Row.

At setup:

Reveal 5 City Charters face up.
The Empire may found at most 4 of them.
Cities are not in the Empire Deck.

Each City Charter has:

name
founding cost
slots
starting tag or local rule
production/storage
unlock condition
