new rules: each player during plotting phase must play at least 1 card. He can play one covered card in the common pool, between the structure and event cards, and optionally one covered edict card in front of him. So one can play only 1 edict, or 1 edict and 1 streucture or 1 edict and 1 crisis, os only a crisis or structure, but not 1 crisis and 1 structure. This allows having a common secret pool and a personal edict card played in front (still covered). During the reveal phase, edicts are revealed together and checked (in physical game, online game guarantees one cannot play a wrong card), because new rule requires that to play an edict you must be the current minister reported on the edict, or any minister if nothing is reported (general edict, not specific). Instead, the rest is shuffled and then revealed as usual. Then all cards are merged and the minister of the empire decides the order as well. This allows players to play edicts to support their proposed structures/crisis and makes the game a little bit faster. Also, crisis cards are drawn on 1st, 3rd, 5th etc. turns.
